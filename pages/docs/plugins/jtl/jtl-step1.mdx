---
title: "Step 1: Install the MovePayment Plugin in JTL"
---

Install the MovePayment plugin through the JTL Extension Store and bind its license to your shop domain.

## Get the Plugin

1. Log in to your JTL customer account, or create an account if you do not have one.
2. On the JTL website, go to **Stores → Go to the Extension Store**.
3. Search for **Move** and select **Move Payment** under **Product suggestions**.
4. Click **Check out now** and log in to your JTL customer account if prompted.
5. Click **Order now** to order the plugin. Ordering the plugin is free of charge, and you will receive a confirmation email.

## Bind the License to Your Shop

1. In your JTL customer account, open **Orders & Licenses**.
2. Scroll to **Extensions** and click **ExtensionStore**.
3. Select **My extensions** and locate the MovePayment plugin.
4. Open the dropdown menu and select **Bind domain**.
5. Enter your shop domain exactly as requested by the instructions in the dialog, then click **Bind domain**.

## Install in JTL

1. Log in to your **JTL Admin Dashboard**.
2. Go to **PLUG-INS → My purchases**.
3. Find MovePayment under **Bound licenses** and click **Install**.

Once installation completes, continue to [access the plugin settings](/docs/plugins/jtl/jtl-step2).
